# Offer comparison

A read-only view shown after `get_offers`, before any order exists. It shows each requested line, the offer the agent would order and what would happen if it did. Approval happens later, on the approval card.

- **Header:** the petrol Valio Aimo bar with the number of products, the requested date and when the offers expire ("5 products · Mon 23 Nov", "until 23:32").
- **Glance:** one row per requested line; two lines of the same product are two rows. Each row has a status icon: a check (no approval needed for this product), a triangle (needs a look) or a dash (not offered). Then the product, a short reason when something needs a look ("Over limit", "2 days late", "64 of 80 l", "Not available"), the price per litre or kilogram ("€8.40/kg": total net / (quantity × pack size); "—", read out as "price per unit unknown", when the pack size is unknown or not in litres or kilograms; none on a row that is not offered) and a chevron. A pre-check that asks for approval or rejects always shows the triangle, even without a reason code. The server marks every late or partial offer `AVAILABILITY_DIFFERS`; the row then says what differs ("2 days late", "64 of 80 l").
- **One product open:** what its own pre-check says, then the links "Details" and "Other options (N)". Each offer is pre-checked on its own, so an offer over the limit names its own amount: "Needs your approval: €14.10 over your order limit. Only 179 of 200 kg can be delivered." Only one product is open at a time, and its content follows its row. (The row would show the catalogue name under the buyer's own words, once request lines carry a label.)
- **Other options:** the agent's pick, marked "Pick", then substitutes, best first, with the rank in words and how they differ ("−2 pts reliability · €0.10/L dearer"; a difference that rounds to nothing is left out). A substitute covers the shortfall of its line, not the whole line. It has a "Use" link unless its pre-check would reject it. Its name opens its own details. "Ask for something else" ends the list.
- **Details:** totals (net, and gross incl. VAT and deposits when there are deposits), deposits, amount, pack, price per l/kg, delivery date, order cut-off, stock, reliability, CO2 (product-group average, kg CO2-eq/kg), origin and the approval. A missing value reads "No data", never 0.
- **Under the products:** each product is checked on its own, so a line says the whole order is checked again when it is placed, by how much the whole order is over the limit when the limit is known, and that picks that would be rejected are not counted.
- **Steering:** "Use", "Ask for something else" and "Try again" (for an offer whose pre-check did not answer) post a user message to the chat through the host (`ui/message`); the agent answers it. "Use" asks for the missing amount only: "For the missing 16 l of Test milk 1 l, use Test organic milk 1 l." The view never orders or approves. The link stays the same button: it reads "Asked ✓" once the chat took the message, and the other steering links wait while a message is on its way. A message the chat did not take within 10 s shows why under its product, and is logged. When the source cannot post a chat message (no `message`), the steering links are not shown.
- **No offers:** the shared shell reports a result without offers as empty. This view keeps the products left out and lists each with its reason.

The order limit comes from `get_mandate`, as on the approval card, and is used only while that mandate is active and is the one (id and version) the offers were made under. Otherwise, or when it cannot be read (logged), the sentences name no amount.

With the app running, open `/views/harness?view=offer-comparison` to inspect the fixture states: `loading`, `empty`, `error`, `offers` and `substitutes`. The fixtures use invented products.

Screenshots (400 px, chat width): `screenshots/offers-*.png`. `offers-open-fi.png` is the same state in Finnish.

Every text this view owns is in `strings.ts`, in English and Finnish; reason sentences come from the approval card's strings, units from its parts, and the loading, empty and error states from the shell. The view follows the language the shared shell picks (the tool input, the host, then the browser); in the harness, `?lang=fi` overrides it.
